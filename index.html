What I believe

People are the best and the worst part of this job.

They come up with brilliant solutions and create plenty of problems along the way. Everyone carries a whole world around in their head and assumes the rest of us can see it. A lot of what I do is help people see each other's worlds a bit more clearly.

How I work

Curious, committed, and a bit of fun.

Generative research is my favourite

I love getting underneath the statistics and assumptions to find the behaviours and attitudes that actually explain them. Honestly, I find it quite poetic.

Asking better questions

A lot of my job happens before any research starts. I help stakeholders work out what they really need to know, and I'm always up for a good conversation about ideas and strategy.

Bringing users into the room

Whatever we're discussing, I make sure the people we're designing for are part of the conversation, even when they can't be there themselves.

Making it enjoyable

Interviews, playbacks, workshops: I try to make all of them fun, because people learn more when they're having a good time. I joke a lot, and I take the work very seriously. Those two things get along fine.
